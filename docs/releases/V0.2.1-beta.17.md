# Chordrift v0.2.1-beta.17

Beta.17 hardens the hosted service against managed-PostgreSQL quota and
availability incidents. Auth0 can still prove a user's identity while the
identity store is unavailable; that dependency failure is now returned as HTTP
503 instead of the misleading HTTP 403 access-denied response. Genuine missing,
expired, or unauthorized sessions retain their existing 401/403 behavior.

The durable worker no longer exits repeatedly when PostgreSQL refuses a
connection, rejects its first schema query, or becomes unavailable while the
queue is being polled. It remains alive and retries with bounded, secret-free
exponential backoff. Recovering the dependency does not authorize provider
work: queued commands retain their normal typed review and authorization
boundaries.

The incident and recovery gates are recorded in the daily-driver edge-case
ledger and operator recovery guide. Contract 1.6 and schema 52 are unchanged;
the release includes no database migration and performs no Spotify write.
