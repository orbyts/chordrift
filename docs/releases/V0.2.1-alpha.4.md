# Chordrift v0.2.1-alpha.4

This prerelease adds five reviewed Indian-library destinations and makes an
explicitly approved manual destination publishable before its first track.

## New destinations

- **Raga Meridian** — North Indian/Hindustani classical music.
- **Kaveri Resonance** — South Indian/Carnatic classical music.
- **Celluloid Mehfil** — Indian film songs released through 1979.
- **Cinema Monsoon** — Indian film music released from 1980 onward.
- **Unscripted Rasa** — Indian non-film music.

Only four already-reviewed classic Hindi tracks seed Celluloid Mehfil. The
other new destinations begin empty so the listener can move their first tracks
directly in Spotify and let ordinary maintenance record those choices without
speculative bulk classification.

## Planner and safety

- Planner v11 creates approved empty manual destinations while continuing to
  suppress empty generated clusters.
- Artwork upload operations now have matching playlist creations in the same
  immutable plan.
- The reviewed plan contains five creates, five artwork uploads, four additions
  to Celluloid Mehfil, and four matching removals from Rasa Archive. It contains
  no exclusions, retirements, or unrelated membership changes.
- Re-evaluate retirement is complete: the current Spotify inventory and the
  database invariant report both show zero active surfaces and tracks while
  historical Neon evidence remains preserved.

## Artwork

Drift Atlas v5 preserves all earlier approved covers and adds five reviewable
masters. The retired Re-evaluate background is reused for Raga Meridian. The
selected Celluloid Mehfil cover is monochrome; its earlier color background and
labeled cover remain under `studies/` rather than being discarded.

Chordrift does not depend on Codex image generation at runtime. Development
creates label-free masters with an image generator, while Chordrift owns exact
title rendering, hashes, immutable batch validation, approval, and publication.
A future automatic generator must sit behind a provider-neutral port and keep
human approval before provider upload.

## Install

```console
$ cargo install chordrift --version 0.2.1-alpha.4 --locked --force
$ rehash
$ chordrift --version
$ chordrift capabilities --require maintenance.unified-workflow.v1
```

This alpha does not change the V021-01 through V021-06 hosted-authority goal or
the intended v0.2.1 final daily-driver release.

Direct moves of already-known tracks between managed playlists are supported.
Destination-only intake of a previously unknown track is intentionally deferred
to alpha.5; until then use Liked Songs or a named intake playlist for new music.
